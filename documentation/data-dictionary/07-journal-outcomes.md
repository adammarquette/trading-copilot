# §7. Journal & outcomes

Part of the [data dictionary](../data-dictionary.md) — see it for the ERD, the **Conventions** that
govern every domain, and the routing table. Cited elsewhere as **data dictionary §7**.

> **Storage codes:** `REL` relational · `TS` time-series (Timescale hypertable) · `VEC` vector.
> **Traces** cites the requirement (`R-#`), ADR, or `gh#N` the row answers to.

| Entity | Key fields | Storage | Traces |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trade** | originating suggestion, instrument, side, entry / exit, size, realized P&L, R multiple, strategy, fills, mode, annotations. **Implemented — journal spine** (gh#7): account FK + suggestion FK (null on deletion), instrument, side/size, entry/exit prices, realized P&L, mode (≠ `undeclared` CHECK — practice results never blend into live results — but **not** trigger-guarded: a trade closes after placement, when the declaration may have moved on; the placement-time guard lives on Order/Suggestion), closedAt. **Written in production** (gh#731): the `AccountEventStreamHost` flat `PositionEvent` reconstructs the round trip from its `Fill`s and writes one row with size-weighted entry/exit, a signed tick-value-aware realized P&L (`InstrumentSpec.RealizedPnL`), mode and suggestion FK **copied from the entry `Order`** (placement-time truth). `(closingFillId, openingFillId)` — FKs to the leg's **final** closing `Fill` and its **opening** `Fill`, **unique (filtered on both non-null)** — is the natural key that makes the writer idempotent: a replayed flat event cannot double-count the day into the daily governor. `closingFillId` alone stopped being unique once a **spanning exit** could retire two legs (gh#759, ADR-0022), so the pair is the key; pre-#759 rows carry only the old single closing key (null `openingFillId`) and stay out of the filtered index. **Only that index's unique violation is read as the benign replay** (gh#747); every other write fault (an FK/CHECK breach, any provider fault) **propagates** to the outer host rather than being swallowed as a skip, so a genuinely lost `Trade` surfaces (the day under-reports visibly) instead of vanishing silently — the same drop-a-row failure class gh#731 exists to close. Indexed `{accountId, closedAt}` for the two live readers (`DailyRealizedReader`, `ConsistencyWindowReader`), which **filter to the account's current `mode`** (R-14, gh#746): the CHECK keeps `undeclared` out of the table, and the readers keep a practice trade on a now-live account from feeding its live daily-governor / consistency limit — completing "practice never blends into live" one layer above the gate (`mode` stays out of the index — a cheap residual at single-operator volume). **Scope:** the writer composes **one row per opening leg** under **FIFO pairing** (gh#759, ADR-0022) — a leg is one opening fill and the closing fills that retire it, and a single exit that **spans** two legs is **split** across them — so **scale-in with partial exit and stop-and-reverse now journal** (their realized P&L reaches the daily governor, each leg's summing to the whole), no longer refused. **Refuse-don't-guess** survives only for genuine ambiguity (an unclassifiable fill side, or an open-from-flat / same-instant opposite-side tie whose direction is undecidable with no venue sequence): those write no row. A window that **does not yet reconcile to flat** — the flat event beat its closing fill into the stream (independent, unordered venue callbacks) — is **not** refused but **deferred** and retried when a fill for the account lands (gh#748, a `deferred` outcome), so a late-arriving fill no longer loses the round trip; the in-memory register survives reconnects, and the restart / never-arrives edge stays with gh#722's reconcile sweep. **Deferred:** R multiple, strategy, per-fill composition, annotations (R-8/R-9) | REL | R-8, R-9 |
| **TradeFeedback** | trade, comment, tags, emotional state, **awaiting-review** flag, author (operator / AI), ts | REL | R-8, R-6 |
| **Outcome** | suggestion / trade, resolution (win / loss / **no-fill-scratch** / expired), simulated?, calibration (predicted vs realized), **`training_excluded`**, **`hidden_from_user`**, `deleted` — the three independent R-15 flags, defined once under *Conventions* above. **Implemented — the model + resolution + R-15 semantics** (gh#832): `Outcome` (IUserOwned, R-20) carries a nullable **`TradeId`** (FK, **cascade** — an outcome dies with its trade, which dies with its account, so account removal carries it away and never strands a trade-only row; **uniquely indexed, filtered on non-null** — the ERD's `Trade ||--o| Outcome` zero-or-one is DB-enforced, so a retry or write-path bug can't mint two outcomes for one trade, the `StopPlanRecord.OrderId` / `SuggestionDisposition.SuggestionId` 1:1-FK posture) and **`SuggestionId`** (FK, **set-null** — survives suggestion deletion, the same posture `Trade.SuggestionId` takes, so the R-9 lineage is not silently erased), a **`Resolution`** (DB-checked `<> Unknown`), `Simulated`, the nullable **calibration pair** (`PredictedRewardRisk` / `RealizedRewardRisk` — a stored **seam**; the untaken-suggestion simulation that populates it is [J2]/[J3]), and the three R-15 flags **set only through the entity's methods** (private setters) — `SoftDelete()` turns on all three together (the reversible combined shortcut), `Restore()` reverses, and training-exclusion / visibility are each settable alone, so the independence R-15 exists for can't be re-collapsed. **`OutcomeResolutionPolicy`** derives the resolution as a **pure function** (as the FIFO pairing #759 is): a closed trade's **signed** P&L → win (>0) / loss (<0) / **no-fill-scratch** (exactly flat — a break-even round trip), an unfilled suggestion → expired or scratched; **refuse-don't-guess** returns nothing for a closed trade carrying no result or a bad-cast basis. **`OutcomeQueries`** are the flag-honouring reads (`IQueryable` extensions): the default journal view (`Visible`) omits soft-deleted **and** hidden rows, a soft-delete toggle returns the **inclusive** figure so the honest picture stays recoverable, and the training read drops `training_excluded` **and** soft-deleted rows (the flags being independent, it can't lean on soft-delete having left `training_excluded` set). `CK_Outcomes_ParentPresent` keeps at least one parent; `CK_Outcomes_Resolution_NotUnknown` keeps the resolution real. **Implemented — the writer + removal I/O** (gh#909): the closed-trade **`OutcomeJournalService`** composes one row per closed `Trade` lacking an outcome (via `OutcomeResolutionPolicy`), owner-scoped and **idempotent** on the `TradeId` unique index (its name pinned by a model-metadata test, the gh#747 posture), driven by a hosted per-pass sweep; the R-20 **`/outcomes`** endpoints serve the read (with the report toggle inclusive vs. exclusive of soft-deleted rows) and the R-15 removals — soft-delete / restore, the two independent toggles, and the confirmed **hard delete** — the endpoint + its audit fact (a new `OutcomeHardDeleted` action, §12) — which for now **refuses a trade-derived outcome** (a 409): the writer would recompose the row, so soft-delete is its removal, and functional hard delete awaits a record with no live re-deriving source (the untaken path, gh#939). **Deferred:** composing outcomes for **terminal unfilled suggestions** — the untaken path (no `TradeId`) needs a `SuggestionId` idempotency index and must settle the set-null-vs-`CK_Outcomes_ParentPresent` orphan, so it lands with the calibration-population + report surface at [J2]/[J3] | REL | R-9, R-15 |
