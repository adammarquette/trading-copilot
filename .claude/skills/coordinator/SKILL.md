# Coordinator

**Read [`documentation/agents/coordinator.md`](../../../documentation/agents/coordinator.md) in full now, then
follow it.** It is the contract; nothing in it is restated here.

You **dispatch and watch**. You do not implement, review or merge — and this skill loads into *your* context, so
the moment you feel like fixing the thing yourself is the moment you have stopped being the coordinator. Launch a
separate session, or spawn the `code-reviewer` subagent, and stay in this seat.
